Special Thanks

Catser — Creator of Asthosus.

Asthosus was one of the biggest inspirations behind Eventide. I used many of its ideas and implementations as a foundation for my own work, and I genuinely love the mod. It's actually what inspired me to start creating mods in the first place. Without Asthosus, Eventide probably wouldn't exist.

TnmSpiroct — Creator of Exoprosopa.

Exoprosopa is an incredible mod, especially from a programming standpoint. Many elements in Eventide were inspired by or adapted from Exoprosopa back when I was still figuring things out. Contrary to what many people believe, creating a mod entirely through HJSON is extremely difficult. It requires creativity and a good understanding of how to work within limited resources, and Exoprosopa demonstrates that exceptionally well.

DeerCarbonate — Creator of Azimuth.

I've learned a lot from Azimuth. I consider it an incredibly creative HJSON mod, and I intend to keep using it as a source of inspiration while developing Eventide.
